ABOUT ME

Exploring biomolecular dynamics through computation

I am a PhD researcher in the Department of Biotechnology at the Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad, working in computational biophysics and molecular simulations under the supervision of Dr. Himanshu Joshi in the Molecular Simulation Lab .

My research focuses on understanding the structure, dynamics, and molecular interactions of complex biomolecular systems. I use atomistic and coarse-grained molecular dynamicsa and enhanced sampling, approaches to investigate protein–ligand, protein–protein, protein–membrane, and nucleic-acid dynamics and its interactions with drug molecules.

I am particularly interested in combining molecular simulations with machine learning to improve sampling, develop molecular models, and extract biologically meaningful information from complex simulation data.

HSA • ICG Dye • Gold Nanoclusters • AA & CG MD

Probing the Dynamical Interaction of Gold Nanoparticles and ICG Dye with Albumin Proteins Using Multiscale MD Simulations

Investigated the molecular interactions and dynamics of Human Serum Albumin (HSA) with gold nanoclusters and Indocyanine Green (ICG) dye using all-atom and coarse-grained molecular dynamics simulations. The study involved all-atom simulations of HSA with a gold nanocluster and HSA with ICG dye, complemented by coarse-grained simulations to explore protein–nanoparticle interactions at larger length and time scales.

Protein • Membrane

NaV1.6 Ion Permeation in a Neuronal Membrane Environment

We investigate Na⁺ permeation through the human NaV1.6 voltage-gated sodium channel using atomistic molecular dynamics, steered molecular dynamics (SMD), and umbrella sampling. The NaV1.6 structure was embedded in a heterogeneous lipid bilayer designed to mimic the lipid composition of neuronal membranes, including cholesterol, POPC, POPE, POPS, sphingomyelin (PSM), and PIP2. SMD was used to generate the ion-translocation pathway, followed by umbrella sampling to calculate the potential of mean force (PMF). This approach enables us to investigate how the native-like membrane environment, pore dynamics, and ion–residue interactions contribute to Na⁺ permeation.

RNA • Molecular Dynamics

Conformational Dynamics of G4C2 Repeat RNA

Using all-atom equilibrium and steered molecular dynamics (SMD) simulations, we investigated the folding, conformational polymorphism, and force-induced unfolding of C9orf72-associated G4C2 repeat RNA. The RNA samples diverse metastable conformations, including hairpin, knot, and G-quadruplex-like structures, with increasing repeat length enhancing conformational flexibility. Simulations of the G-quadruplex revealed distinct unfolding pathways involving strand slippage and unzipping, providing molecular insights into the structural dynamics of ALS/FTD-associated RNA.

DNA • Topology

SketchDNA: A GUI-Enabled Toolkit for Multiscale Modeling of Topological DNA Structures

SketchDNA (SDNA) a GUI-enabled topological DNA design tool, that facilitates a user-friendly and quick interface to create all-atom and coarse-grained models of DNA structures with tunable topological parameters. Building on the modular, open-source framework of SDNA, the software can be readily integrated with both emerging and existing DNA design platforms, such as oxDNA, mrDNA etc. We demonstrate the utility of SDNA computational framework by simulating conformational dynamics of three representative topological DNA structures, minicircles, catanenes, and Borromean rings.

Machine Learning

Machine Learning-Based Force-Field Refinement for Accurate Drug-Like Molecular Simulations

Developing data-driven approaches to improve molecular force-field parameterization, with a focus on CGenFF. A curated dataset of CGenFF-parameterized molecules is constructed using pyCHARMM, while Open Babel and RDKit are used for molecular structure processing and generation of atom-level molecular descriptors. Multiple machine learning approaches are being investigated for the prediction of charges and bonded parameters (bonds, angles, and dihedrals). Free Energy Perturbation (FEP) calculations are also being employed to evaluate solvation free energies and assess the quality of the predicted parameters.
