ABOUT ME

Exploring biomolecular dynamics through computation

I am a PhD researcher in the Department of Biotechnology at the Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad, working in the area of computational biophysics and molecular simulations.

My research focuses on understanding how biomolecules explore their conformational landscapes and interact with other molecules. I use molecular dynamics, enhanced sampling, and multiscale computational approaches to investigate these processes.

I am particularly interested in combining molecular simulations with machine learning to improve sampling, develop molecular models, and extract biologically meaningful information from complex simulation data.

RESEARCH

Research Interests

My research combines molecular simulations and data-driven approaches to understand biomolecular structure, dynamics, and interactions.

01

RNA Conformational Dynamics

Investigating RNA folding, structural transitions, conformational landscapes, and interactions with proteins and small molecules.

  • Molecular dynamics simulations
  • Enhanced sampling
  • RNA folding pathways
02

DNA Structure & Topology

Studying the dynamics and topology of unusual DNA structures and their interactions with DNA-binding proteins.

  • Topological DNA structures
  • DNA-protein interactions
  • Groove dynamics
03

Protein Dynamics

Exploring protein conformational dynamics, ligand interactions, and molecular mechanisms using atomistic simulations.

  • Protein conformational dynamics
  • Protein-ligand interactions
  • Membrane proteins
04

Machine Learning & Molecular Simulation

Developing data-driven approaches to improve molecular simulations and extract meaningful conformational information.

  • ML-assisted molecular simulations
  • Force-field development
  • Dimensionality reduction
